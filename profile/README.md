# DeepNile

AI that anyone can use without money.

Today the biggest AI models live in data centres that a handful of companies can afford, and everybody else rents
them by the month. Meanwhile home computers sit idle most of the day. Joined together, they can run those same
models. DeepNile joins them. Its code will be open, no company sits in the middle, and you pay with your computer's
spare time instead of money.

Why it matters: [Who pays for central AI](https://deepnile.org/why/#cost), with every figure's source.
